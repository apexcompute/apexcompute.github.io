Finite Elements from Scratch

An introductory course on the finite element method. It is for people who know a little calculus, can multiply a matrix by a vector and can read simple Python. No mechanics background is needed.

Episodes

  1. Ep. 1 Change one spring: which cells of K change?

    Three springs in a chain, a wall on the left, 60 N pulling on the right. Every finite element program ends up solving K u = f. Here you build that K yourself, one spring at a time.

  2. Ep. 2 The weak form: why we rewrite the heat equation

    An aluminum bar, 4 cm long, makes 80 W of heat along its length; both ends are held at 20 °C. A broken line of two straight pieces can't go into its equation. Rewrite the equation, and it can. Here you find the middle temperature yourself, by balancing one hat.

  3. Ep. 3 Exact at the nodes? Finite elements in Python

    The same heated bar as episode 2 (4 cm, 80 W, both ends at 20 °C), now cut into 4 and then 8 elements and solved by a short program. Guess a node, then cut the bar finer yourself and watch where the error hides.
